# `Enum.LightingStyle`

Enum used by `Class.Lighting.LightingStyle` to indicate the artistic intent
behind lighting in the experience.

This enum indicates the artistic intent behind lighting in the experience. It
is used by the `Class.Lighting.LightingStyle` property.

The `Enum.LightingStyle` enum has 2 item(s).

| Name | Value | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| `Enum.LightingStyle.Realistic` | 0 | The most advanced and realistic lighting and shadows Roblox can deliver. |
| `Enum.LightingStyle.Soft` | 1 | A flat, retro-Roblox look with softer lights and shadows. |
