# `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode`

Overwrites the player's camera movement mode setting on a computer.

Overwrites the player's camera movement mode setting on a computer.

The `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode` enum has 5 item(s).

| Name | Value | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode.UserChoice` | 0 | The camera will move based on the player's settings. |
| `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode.Classic` | 1 | Camera tracks the player but will not automatically rotate if the player walks left or right. |
| `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode.Follow` | 2 | Camera tracks the player and automatically rotates if the player walks left or right. |
| `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode.Orbital` | 3 | The camera has a fixed Y position, but can be rotated around the player. |
| `Enum.DevComputerCameraMovementMode.CameraToggle` | 4 | The camera toggles between locked and free rotation with the right mouse button. |
